ClassDojo has seven brands under one roof: Dojo (the parent), ClassDojo, Districts, Tutor, Sparks, Islands and Plus. Their rules were spread across Figma files and decks, so most brand questions landed in my Slack DMs and the answers got buried there.

Now every brand gets a page in [Brand Studio](/play/brand-studio), and they all share one structure so you only have to learn it once. Strategy, logo, mascot, colors, gradient, icons, illustration, photo, and a gallery of the brand out in the wild.

## Made to be copied

Every color has its values ready to copy. Logos sit on their approved backgrounds with clear space marked. Each page has a Download assets button that zips up the kit in the formats people actually ask for.

Type gets its own page with Melun and Ginto, the raw scales, and the sizes for desktop, tablet and mobile, so a designer and an engineer are looking at the same numbers.

## For agents too

The newest bit is an endpoint that hands back a brand's palette and logo rules as plain text, along with the page link to cite. So when someone asks Claude (or any agent) to make something on-brand, it reads the real rules instead of guessing from a screenshot.

## How it came together

The first layouts went up in early March. I scrapped them, rebuilt the shared pieces in late April, and filled in all seven brands that same week. The shared pieces are a strategy row, color boards, logo and icon grids, section nav, and a scroll progress bar that takes each brand's color.

These pages ended up feeding the other tools too. The [Tutor ad builder](/play/tutor-ads) and its art-director skill both point back here for the rules.
