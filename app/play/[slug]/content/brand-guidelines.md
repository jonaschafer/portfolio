ClassDojo has seven brands under one roof: the parent brand Dojo, ClassDojo, Districts, Tutor, Sparks, Islands and Plus. Their rules were spread across Figma files and decks, so most brand questions came to me in Slack, and the answers got buried there.

Now each brand has a page in [Brand Studio](/play/brand-studio), all built on one structure so you learn it once. Strategy, logo, mascot, colors, gradient, icons, illustration, photo and a gallery of the brand in use.

## Built to be copied

Every color has its values ready to copy. Logos sit on their approved backgrounds with clear space shown. Each page has a Download assets button that zips the brand kit: logos, icons and the rest, in the formats people ask for.

Type gets its own page. Melun and Ginto, the raw scales, and the semantic sizes for desktop, tablet and mobile, so a designer and an engineer read the same numbers.

## For agents too

The newest piece is an endpoint that returns a brand's palette and logo rules as plain text, with the page URL to cite. When someone asks Claude or another agent to make something on-brand, it can read the real rules instead of guessing from a screenshot.

## How it came together

The first layouts went up in early March. I scrapped them, rebuilt the shared components in late April, and filled in all seven brands that week. The shared pieces are a strategy row, color boards, logo and icon grids, section nav, and a scroll progress bar in each brand's own color.

These pages also became the source for the other tools. The [Tutor ad builder](/play/tutor-ads) and its art-director skill both point back here for the rules.
