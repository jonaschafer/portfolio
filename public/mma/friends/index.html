For friends and family

Jon's jaw surgery.

What's happening, where I'm at, and how to help.

What's going on

On Friday, October 2 I'm having MMA surgery (maxillomandibular advancement) at OHSU. The surgeons cut both my upper and lower jaw, move them forward, and hold them in place with small titanium plates and screws. Moving the jaws forward opens up the airway behind the tongue. The goal is to fix my sleep apnea.

I won't be wired shut. I'll have rubber bands on my braces for a few weeks, a very swollen face, a numb lip and chin, and a no-chewing rule for six weeks. Full recovery takes about three months, and the final result shows up around a year.

The journey

How to help

Text, don't call

Talking is hard and tiring for the first couple of weeks. Texts are great. Don't expect a fast reply.

Check in around week 1

The surgeons say almost everyone feels low or regretful in the first two weeks. A note that week goes a long way.

Visit from week 2

Short visits are welcome once I'm past the first week. I'll be puffy and mumbly. Please don't make me laugh too hard.

Come walk with me

Walking is the main exercise for the first month. Company makes it better.

Send recommendations

Shows, movies, podcasts, audiobooks. There will be a lot of couch time.

Mind the face

No hugs that press on my face, and nothing that could bump my jaw for three months. A knock can shift the bones.
